# Privacy Policy for Play2Learn (Math Monsters)

**Last Updated: June 2026**

At **Play2Learn**, we are committed to protecting the privacy of the children and families who use our apps. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection and use of information in the "Math Monsters" mobile application.

## 1. No Personal Information Collection
We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal information from the users of our app. 
- We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.
- We do not require a login or the creation of a user account to play the game.

## 2. No Data Tracking or Analytics
We do **not** use any third-party analytics or tracking software. 
- We do not track how long you play or which levels you prefer.
- Your progress (completed levels and high scores) is stored **locally** on your device only and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

## 3. No Third-Party Advertising
There are **no advertisements** in Math Monsters. Children can enjoy a focused learning experience without interruptions or exposure to external marketing.

## 4. In-App Purchases
The app contains a one-time In-App Purchase to unlock all levels. 
- All payment processing is handled securely by the **Google Play Store**. 
- We do not have access to your credit card or financial information.
- We recommend that parents use the "Password Requirement" setting on the Google Play Store to prevent unauthorized purchases by children.

## 5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
Our app is designed to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 6. External Links
There are **no external links** (social media, websites, or other apps) within the gameplay areas of the app to ensure children remain within the safe environment of the game.
